# Landing page B: the morning gate (landing-guard/index.html)

## Scope and mode
Persuade. A second, security-first landing page on branch `landing-guard`, kept beside the first one (landing/).
Audience and goal as PRODUCT.md: the faculty administration, to be impressed. Arabic first, FR and EN.

## Asked for
More security-oriented than page A; a 3D model; focus on the registers and the security guard, not the ID card.

## Direction contract
THESIS: The page is the first hour of the morning shift at the faculty gate, seen in 3D. A uniformed guard stands by his booth in low early sun and watches the visitor's cursor, as does the security camera on the booth roof; the camera moves from the guard to the register shelf in his booth, to the barrier, and up over the whole post. It refuses the dark neon "cyber" look and page A's pastel plaster.
OWN-WORLD: Morning light: pale sky #cfe2ec to a warm horizon #f6dcb8, low sun #ffd9a0 throwing long cool shadows. Whitewashed sand walls #e3d2b6, pale stone paving, security navy #13294b for the uniform, the text ink and the panels' rules, signal amber #e09a2b as the one accent, barrier red #c8202e with white stripes, ledger spines in oxblood, ledger green, navy, ochre, slate and teal. Solid warm-white text panels #f7f3ec. Matte materials, real sun shadows, no glows. Alexandria display, Tajawal body, Martian Mono for log entries.
STORY: The visitor sees that every shift, visitor, incident and handover is written into numbered registers that never erase, that the guard's work is the product, and that the data never leaves the post. They leave impressed and trust it.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Full-bleed WebGL morning scene: the faculty wall and arch gate with the faculty plaque above it, the whitewashed guard booth with its open counter window, the striped barrier, the guard in front of the booth, his head turning to the cursor, a security camera on the booth roof turning with it, long shadows across the paving. Headline in Alexandria on the inline-start side over a solid warm-white panel; a mono log ticker of sample morning register entries along the bottom.
FORM: One persistent three.js scene behind all sections; scroll moves the camera between shots (guard, shelf, barrier, overview). Brief-pinned direction, roll skipped. seed: none (brief-pinned, roll skipped)
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.

## Assets
Guard: three.js example model Xbot.glb (Mixamo character, from github.com/mrdoob/three.js examples/models/gltf), recoloured as a navy uniform with an authored cap, badge and radio. Everything else is modelled in code.
